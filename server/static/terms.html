Terms & Community Guidelines

Last updated: 29 October 2026

TopicTalk is a community app for Arakkonam — a place to ask questions, review places, find local shops and help each other out. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

You must be 13 or older

TopicTalk is not for children under 13. By signing up, you confirm you're at least 13 years old.

Your account

  • Use your real experience — don't impersonate another person, business or TopicTalk itself.
  • Keep your password private. You're responsible for activity on your account.
  • One person, one account.

What's not allowed

  • Fake content: fake reviews, fake ratings, or posts pretending to be from someone you're not.
  • Harassment or hate: bullying, threats, slurs, or content attacking someone for who they are.
  • Sexual or violent content: nothing sexual, graphic, or that promotes violence.
  • Spam or scams: repeated unwanted posts, fraudulent offers, or misleading shop claims.
  • Sharing others' private information without their consent (addresses, phone numbers, photos, etc.).
  • Anything illegal under Indian law.

Reporting & moderation

You can report any post, comment or user from its "Report" option — reports go to the admin only, and the person reported is never told who reported them. Blocking someone stops them from contacting you or seeing your posts. The admin reviews reports and can remove content or accounts that break these rules.

Shop listings & owner claims

Anyone can add a shop listing with factual, non-fake details. A shop's verified "Owner" badge is only given after the admin has called or visited the shop to confirm who runs it — claiming a shop you don't run, or posting false offers, will get the claim rejected and may get your account removed.

Content you post

You keep ownership of what you post, but you're giving other TopicTalk users permission to view it as part of using the app. Don't post anything you don't have the right to share.

Account removal

You can delete your account anytime from Settings. We may suspend or remove an account that repeatedly breaks these rules, even without prior warning for serious violations.

Changes

We may update these terms as the app grows. Continuing to use TopicTalk after a change means you accept the update.

See also our Privacy Policy.
