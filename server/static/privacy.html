Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

TopicTalk is a community app for Arakkonam. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control or remove it. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers — TopicTalk doesn't run ads.

What we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Username & passwordTo create and secure your account. Passwords are stored as a salted hash, never in plain text.
Email address (if you sign up with email)To verify your account and let you reset your password.
Posts, comments, reviews, photosThe content you choose to share with the community. Visible to whoever the post's audience is.
Group and shop-owner chat messagesTo deliver messages between members of a group or discussion you've joined.
Phone number (only if you claim a shop)So TopicTalk can call to confirm you run the shop before giving you the verified-owner badge. Only you and the admin can see it.
Your chosen city/areaTo show you local discussions, shops and news for that area.
Precise location (only if you tap "Near me")To sort shops and places by distance. It's used for that one search and isn't stored on our server.
Push notification subscriptionTo send you alerts you've opted into (replies, mentions, shop offers, etc.). You can turn this off anytime in Settings.
IP addressUsed briefly for spam/abuse rate-limiting. Not linked to your profile or stored long-term.

Who can see what

  • Public posts, reviews and shop listings are visible to anyone using TopicTalk.
  • Private group messages are visible only to members of that group.
  • Your phone number (shop claims) is visible only to you and the TopicTalk admin.
  • Reports you file are visible only to you and the admin — the person you reported is never told who reported them.

How we use it

We use your information only to run TopicTalk: showing you relevant local content, delivering messages and notifications, verifying shop owners, and keeping the community safe from spam and abuse. We do not sell data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

TopicTalk runs on a single server we operate. Data isn't shared with outside companies except where this policy says so (e.g. sending you an email).

Your choices

  • Delete your account: go to Settings → Delete account inside the app. This removes your login, profile and personal details immediately. See our note on what happens to past posts in the app itself.
  • Notifications: turn any category off in Settings → Notifications.
  • Location: "Near me" location access is optional — you can always browse by picking your area manually instead.
  • Blocking: block anyone from contacting or seeing your posts in Settings or from their profile.

Children

TopicTalk is not directed at children under 13, and we ask everyone to confirm they're 13 or older when creating an account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach the TopicTalk admin through the app.
